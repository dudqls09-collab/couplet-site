Couplet Privacy Policy

Data stored

Couplet stores your settings, subtitle choices for each title, and subtitle timing adjustments in Chrome's local storage on your device.

Subtitles

Couplet loads Netflix subtitles from the addresses provided by the Netflix page. Subtitle files you open are read on your device and are not uploaded.

Data shared

Couplet does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It uses no analytics, advertising, or servers.
